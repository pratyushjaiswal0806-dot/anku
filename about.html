questions people ask over coffee

01so, agency or in-house?

Both, and freelance too. Agencies taught me rigour and pace, in-house taught me what the business actually needs, and freelance taught me to do all of it with a smaller team.

02what brands do you work best with?

Ones with a real problem to solve. Enterprise to kids’ snacks, gin to oncology: the category matters less than the curiosity.

03can you handle sensitive subjects?

It’s some of the work I’m proudest of: cancer awareness for amanté, a women’s oncology centre launch for Apollo, an anti-tobacco campaign. Trust first, cleverness second.

04do you just “post on instagram”?

Respectfully, no. Strategy, scripting, creator negotiations, production logistics, packaging direction, and the reporting deck at the end. Ten reels, two languages, fifteen days, on budget, for one.

05anything you’re still learning?

Always. “Much to learn, you still have,” as a small green philosopher once said. I take that as a job description.
